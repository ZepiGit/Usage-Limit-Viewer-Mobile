Connect a supported subscription to read its usage windows and reset times, as a list or as dials.

Four themes — Warm dark, Pure black, Cool graphite and Light paper — plus bundled fonts. The Claude Code figure follows how much of the account's headline is left.

  <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="docs/images/readme/11-themes-dark.png">

## Your data

The app talks straight to each service with your own account. There is no server, no proxy and no tracking; keys stay in the Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain.
